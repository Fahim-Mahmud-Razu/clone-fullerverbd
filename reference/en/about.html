About Us
A global manufacturer and supplier of leather safety goods, committed to delivering high-quality personal protective equipment and reliable service to customers around the world.
Who We Are

Experience, capacity and quality in every glove.

FULLEVER BD LIMITED is a global manufacturer and supplier of leather safety goods. It is our company goal to ensure countries across the world have access to a range of high-quality personal protective equipment to keep vital workers safe.

Our factory is the largest leather glove manufacturer in Bangladesh, and we are still rapidly expanding. We are experts in leather goods with over 40 years of experience producing gloves.

We currently employ over 1,200 skilled workers , enabling us to produce 100,000 dozen of gloves per month

Customer service and consistent high-quality supply are our biggest priorities. We have instituted a Western-style management approach to provide professional and responsive service to our partners.

We record and track leather quality, inventory and production data so that every glove we send out is thoroughly inspected.

Dedicated individuals manage the US, European, Japanese and Australian markets, allowing us to communicate quickly with customers across the globe.

Our Vision

Responsible growth with a global purpose.

Our vision focuses on minimizing delays and unknowns with our shipments. We have achieved this by sourcing 95% of our raw materials locally in Bangladesh.

By locating our supply chain within Bangladesh, we avoid many of the issues currently occurring with shipment costs and delays. This allows us to supply orders very quickly from when we receive them. Our factory is only 6–7 hours from the nearest seaport in Chittagong, allowing easy access for delivering our products.

As a company, it is our goal to become the largest safety goods supplier across the globe. We continue to grow and innovate, discovering new ways to make a better and more efficient glove so that we can provide the highest quality products at a competitive cost.

We believe that the right partners, FULLEVER BD LIMITED can advance the rapidly growing PPE industry.
