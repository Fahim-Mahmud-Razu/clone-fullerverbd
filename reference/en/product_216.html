Product Detail: 

Key Features of Heat Resistant Leather Welding Gloves:

Heat Resistant Leather: Crafted from full heat resistant leather, these gloves provide exceptional protection against high temperatures. This premium leather is specifically treated to withstand the intense heat and sparks typical of welding and other high-temperature tasks, ensuring your hands remain safe and comfortable.

Heat Resistant Liner: Inside the gloves, a full heat resistant wool liner adds an extra layer of protection, effectively insulating your hands from extreme temperatures. This liner enhances the gloves' overall heat resistance, making them suitable for prolonged use in demanding environments.

Cotton Fabric Liner: For added comfort, the gloves feature a full cotton fabric liner. This soft, breathable layer ensures that your hands stay cool and comfortable, even during extended periods of use. The cotton liner also wicks away moisture, preventing sweat buildup and maintaining a comfortable fit.

Aramid Thread: The gloves are sewn with aramid thread, known for its exceptional strength and heat resistance. This high-performance thread ensures that the gloves maintain their integrity under extreme conditions, providing reliable protection and longevity.

Wing Thumb Design: The wing thumb design enhances the flexibility and dexterity of the gloves, allowing for better movement and control. This ergonomic feature reduces hand fatigue and improves overall comfort, making it easier to handle tools and materials with precision.

Four Tips Reinforcement: The gloves are reinforced at the fingertips with four tips design, offering additional durability and protection in high-wear areas. This reinforcement ensures that the gloves can withstand the rigors of heavy-duty use, extending their lifespan and maintaining their protective qualities.

Versatile Applications:

Heat and Welding Applications: Specifically designed for use in heat and welding applications, these gloves provide reliable protection against high temperatures, sparks, and molten metal. Whether you're welding, cutting, or performing other heat-intensive tasks, these gloves ensure your hands remain safe and secure.

Industrial and Professional Use: Ideal for industrial and professional settings, these gloves are suitable for a wide range of applications beyond welding. From metalworking to foundry work, the gloves offer superior heat resistance and durability for various high-temperature environments.

Product Specifications:

Material: Full heat resistant leather for exceptional heat protection

Liner: Full heat resistant wool liner and full cotton fabric liner for added comfort and insulation

Thread: Sewn with aramid thread for enhanced durability and heat resistance

Design: Wing thumb for improved flexibility and dexterity

Reinforcement: Four tips reinforcement for additional durability in high-wear areas

Color: Natural leather with reinforced stitching for a professional look

Care Instructions:

To ensure the longevity and performance of Our Premium Heat Resistant Leather Welding Gloves, follow these care instructions:

Cleaning: Wipe the leather with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid soaking the gloves or using harsh chemicals. The interior lining can be spot cleaned as needed.

Drying: Allow the gloves to air dry naturally, away from direct heat sources and sunlight. Do not wring or tumble dry.

Storage: Store the gloves in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid crushing or folding to maintain their shape and integrity.
