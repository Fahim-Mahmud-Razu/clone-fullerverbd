Product Detail:
Heat Resistant Welding Gloves Key Features:

Cow Red Split Leather (Front & Back): These gloves are constructed from full cow red split leather on both the front and back, providing exceptional durability and resistance to heat, sparks, and abrasions. The high-quality leather offers a robust barrier against external hazards while maintaining flexibility for ease of movement.

Aluminum Liner for Heat Resistance: Inside the gloves, a full aluminum liner offers superior heat resistance. This advanced lining reflects heat away from the hands, ensuring that the wearer remains protected even in extremely high-temperature environments.

Denim Fabric Liner: The addition of a full denim fabric liner enhances the comfort and breathability of the gloves. This liner helps to wick away moisture, keeping the hands dry and comfortable during prolonged use.

Aramid Thread: The gloves are stitched with aramid thread, known for its high strength and heat resistance. This ensures that the seams remain intact and durable even under intense heat and stress, enhancing the overall lifespan of the gloves.

Wing Thumb: The wing thumb design offers improved ergonomics, providing greater flexibility and a natural fit. This design allows for better movement and dexterity, making it easier to handle welding tools and materials.

Four Tips: The four-tip design provides additional reinforcement in key areas, enhancing both durability and protection against wear and tear.

Heat Resistance: These gloves are specifically engineered to resist high temperatures, making them ideal for welding and other heat-intensive applications. The combination of cow split leather and an aluminum liner ensures comprehensive protection against heat.

Cut Resistance: The gloves feature cut-resistant protection on the back side, safeguarding against sharp objects and potential lacerations. This added layer of safety is crucial for handling metal and other sharp materials commonly encountered in welding environments.

Versatile Applications:

Welding: Perfect for professional welders, these gloves offer the necessary protection against heat, sparks, and sharp edges. They are ideal for various welding techniques, including MIG, TIG, and stick welding.

High-Temperature Work: Suitable for any task that involves exposure to high temperatures, such as metalworking, foundry work, and glass blowing. The gloves provide reliable protection and comfort in these demanding environments.

Industrial Safety: These gloves are also ideal for use in other industrial settings where heat and cut hazards are prevalent. They ensure that workers can perform their tasks safely and efficiently.

Product Specifications

Material: Entire cow red split leather (front & back)

Thread: Aramid thread for enhanced durability and heat resistance

Design: Wing thumb and four tips for improved ergonomics and reinforcement

Color: Red with black accents

Care Instructions:

To maintain the quality and performance of Our Premium Heat Resistant Welding Gloves, follow these care instructions:

Cleaning: Gently brush off any dirt or debris. If necessary, wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid soaking the gloves or using harsh chemicals.

Drying: Allow the gloves to air dry naturally, away from direct heat sources and sunlight. Do not wring or tumble dry.

Storage: Store the gloves in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid folding or crushing to maintain their shape and integrity.
