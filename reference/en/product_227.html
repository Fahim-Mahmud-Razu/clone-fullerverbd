Product Detail: 

Introducing our latest innovation in hand protection: the Premium Cut Resistant Safety Gloves. These gloves are expertly crafted by a global leader in leather safety goods, ensuring top-tier quality and performance. Designed for professionals who require the highest level of cut resistance without compromising on comfort and dexterity, these gloves are ideal for various safety applications.

Cut resistant - safety gloves Key Features:

Goat Grain Leather: The gloves are made from premium full goat grain leather, renowned for its exceptional softness, durability, and flexibility. This high-quality leather ensures a comfortable fit while providing robust protection against cuts and abrasions. The natural properties of goat leather also offer a secure, non-slip grip, enhancing your ability to handle tools and materials with precision.

Knitted Cut Level Five Lining: Inside the gloves, a knitted cut level five lining delivers top-tier cut resistance. This advanced lining is engineered to provide the highest level of protection against sharp objects and potential lacerations, ensuring your hands remain safe during demanding tasks.

Black Cotton Piping: The gloves are reinforced with black cotton piping, which not only adds a stylish touch but also enhances the structural integrity of the gloves. This reinforcement ensures that the gloves maintain their shape and durability even under rigorous use.

Cut Level Five Protection: The combination of full goat grain leather and the cut level five lining offers unmatched cut resistance. These gloves are ideal for handling sharp tools, glass, metal, and other hazardous materials, providing comprehensive protection for your hands.

Enhanced Dexterity and Grip: Despite their robust protection, these gloves maintain excellent flexibility and grip. The goat grain leather allows for a natural range of motion, enabling you to perform tasks requiring precision and control without sacrificing safety.

Versatile Applications:

Industrial Safety: Perfect for use in industries such as manufacturing, construction, and metalworking, where cut hazards are a significant concern. These gloves provide reliable protection for workers handling sharp objects and materials.

Glass Handling: Ideal for professionals in the glass industry, offering the necessary cut resistance and dexterity required for safely handling glass panes and shards.

General Maintenance and Repairs: Suitable for various maintenance and repair tasks involving sharp tools and components. These gloves ensure your hands remain safe and agile.

Product Specifications

Material: Full goat grain leather exterior

Lining: Knitted cut level five lining for superior cut resistance

Reinforcement: Black cotton piping for enhanced durability and style

Protection Level: Cut level five

Color: Natural leather with black piping accents

Care Instructions:

To maintain the quality and performance of Our Premium Cut Resistant Safety Gloves, follow these care instructions:

Cleaning: Gently brush off any debris or dust. If necessary, wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid soaking the gloves or using harsh chemicals.

Drying: Allow the gloves to air dry naturally, away from direct heat sources and sunlight. Do not wring or tumble dry.

Storage: Store the gloves in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid crushing or folding to maintain their shape and integrity.
