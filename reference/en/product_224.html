Heat Resistant Welding Gloves

021300302151000

  • Description

Heat Resistant Leather only at palm & front cuff can resist more than 100 deg Celsius, Para-aramid aluminized back (480GSM), Wool lining as heat resistant Liner, Cotton Fabric Liner, Aramid Thread, Wing Thumb, Four Tips.

Available gloves sizing :

Product Detail:

Heat Resistant Leather (Palm & Front Cuff): The palm and front cuff of the gloves are made from specially treated heat resistant leather. This premium material provides excellent protection against high temperatures, ensuring that your hands remain safe from intense heat and sparks during welding and other heat-intensive tasks.

Para-Aramid Aluminized Back (480 GSM): The back of the gloves features para-aramid aluminized fabric with a density of 480 GSM. This high-performance material offers superior heat and cut resistance, reflecting radiant heat and providing an additional layer of protection against sharp objects.

Heat Resistant Liner: Inside the gloves, a full heat resistant wool liner adds an extra layer of protection, ensuring your hands stay cool and safe even during prolonged exposure to high temperatures.

Cotton Fabric Liner: For added comfort, the gloves include a full cotton fabric liner. This soft, breathable lining wicks away moisture, keeping your hands dry and comfortable throughout extended use.

Aramid Thread: The gloves are stitched with aramid thread, known for its exceptional heat resistance and strength. This durable stitching ensures that the seams remain intact even under extreme conditions, providing reliable protection and longevity.

Wing Thumb Design: The ergonomic wing thumb design enhances flexibility and dexterity, allowing for a better grip and easier movement. This feature ensures that you can handle tools and materials with precision and ease.

Four Tips: Reinforced tips on the fingers provide extra durability and protection, enhancing the gloves' resistance to wear and tear and ensuring long-lasting performance.

Versatile Applications:

Heat and Welding Applications: These gloves are specifically designed for high-temperature environments, making them ideal for welding, metalworking, and other heat-intensive tasks. The combination of heat resistant leather and para-aramid aluminized fabric ensures maximum protection against heat and flames.

Cut Resistant Back: The back of the gloves offers additional cut resistance, making them suitable for tasks where both heat and cut protection are essential. This feature ensures comprehensive hand safety in various demanding applications.

General High-Temperature Work: Beyond welding, these gloves are suitable for any job involving high temperatures and cut risks. Whether you’re working in a forge, handling hot materials, or performing maintenance in high-heat environments, these gloves provide the necessary protection.

Product Specifications:

Material: Heat resistant leather on palm and front cuff, para-aramid aluminized fabric (480 GSM) on the back

Liner: Full heat resistant wool liner, full cotton fabric liner

Thread: Aramid thread for high heat resistance and durability

Design Features: Wing thumb for enhanced dexterity, four tips for reinforced protection

Color: Natural leather tones with aluminized back and durable stitching

Care Instructions:

To maintain the quality and performance of Our Premium Heat Resistant Leather Welding Gloves, follow these care instructions:

Cleaning: Gently brush off any debris or dust. If necessary, wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid soaking the gloves or using harsh chemicals.

Drying: Allow the gloves to air dry naturally, away from direct heat sources and sunlight. Do not wring or tumble dry.

Storage: Store the gloves in a cool, dry place when not in use. Avoid crushing or folding to maintain their shape and integrity.
