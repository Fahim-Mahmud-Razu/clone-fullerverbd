## Users
International B2B buyers, importers, and safety equipment procurement managers sourcing certified industrial and leather work gloves directly from a Bangladesh manufacturer. Secondary audiences include overseas distributors, industrial safety officers, and enterprise procurement specialists.

## Product Purpose
Provide a high-fidelity B2B corporate showcase and comprehensive product catalog with direct inquiry/RFQ dispatch (no online checkout). Serves as the primary digital showroom and credibility anchor for MAX GLOVES BD LTD., enabling overseas clients to inspect glove specifications, manufacturing processes, factory facilities, compliance standards, and initiate direct inquiries.

## Positioning
Direct-from-factory industrial glove manufacturing in Bangladesh (corporate office in Dhaka, manufacturing plant in Savar, Hemayetpur). Delivers honest, transparent direct-procurement communication, strict adherence to industrial safety standards (leather welding, mechanics, driver, split, impact, palm, synthetic), and dedicated customer service without intermediary markup or inflated/fabricated claims.

## Operating Context
B2B procurement cycles for industrial personal protective equipment (PPE). Buyers evaluate technical glove specs (leather grades, EN standards, cut/heat resistance, palm reinforcement, sizing), factory compliance credentials, and production capacity before issuing Requests for Quotation (RFQs) via direct email or telephone.
